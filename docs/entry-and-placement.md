# Account entry and a useful starting point

Guest play and Google sign-in are visible at arrival. A learner is a separate
journey, not proof of Google authentication. The account page groups device
guests and the signed-in account's linked learners, with explicit save status.
Returning Google sessions restore without a new popup. Previously selected
linked learners can resume after that identity has been restored. Signing in
does not silently attach a guest; linking names the learner and destination
account and requires confirmation. New learners can explicitly choose cloud
saving in the creation form. Existing profiles and event formats are preserved.

Owner-created email invitations remove the approval wait for invited family
members while retaining the private-family access boundary. See
[account-enrollment.md](account-enrollment.md) for the exact server checks and
deployment prerequisite. An invitation does not send email or grant an owner
role. Existing members are unchanged. Uninvited people can still play locally.

The optional starting-point check begins around the A2 practice material. Two
correct responses move toward harder material; two incorrect/skipped responses
move toward easier material; mixed responses get another sample. It stops at a
neighboring boundary or ten responses. Selection favors unseen material and
samples reading plus constrained language use. Every submitted response is
saved, so leaving/reloading can resume. Results are recalculated from actual
responses, not imported correctness or level labels.

The learner may accept the suggested practice range, retain their current
path, choose a range manually, or begin playing without assessment. Accepted
placement changes the starting study region. It creates no mastery, review
schedule, completed missions, or game rewards, and never changes experience
mode. Subsequent study evidence and targeted review remain authoritative.
Pre-readers have a separate adult-assisted building entry, without a reading
assessment or developmental validity claim.

The 18 accepted probes reuse existing original material: three per range, with
two short reading choices and one constrained language-use response. Sol
reviewed Luna's candidate selection and replaced one answer-exposing item.
Acceptance pins both the research submission and every underlying activity;
build and CI fail if the reviewed pool drifts. These are uncalibrated practice
probes, with easy distractors and limited domain coverage. A C2 practice
suggestion is not evidence of comprehensive C2 ability. Listening, speaking,
open writing, age suitability and learning efficacy have not been validated.

The UI filters other accounts' linked local records and exported backups;
this is privacy within the application, not encryption against the browser's
owner. Guests are shared within the browser. Sign out before leaving a shared
device. Cloud saving remains explicit, and a last-save indicator is cleared
when newer work exists. Provider sign-out failures must offer recovery rather
than falsely claiming the session has ended.

Verification evidence and production status are recorded in PROJECT_STATE.md.
