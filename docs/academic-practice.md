### Format and rights

All new prompts, distractors, explanations, documents and passages are original Atlas material. The named studies, organisations, correspondence and conversations are fictional educational stimuli. External resources informed format decisions only. Original provider papers, scripts, transcripts and recordings are outside these sets.

- IELTS distinguishes checking facts from identifying the writer’s views. FALSE/NO items have contradictory evidence, and NOT GIVEN items identify a missing fact or unstated opinion. Paragraph and heading work uses the current single-choice interface. [IELTS Academic Reading](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading)
- TOEIC Reading Parts 5–7 use incomplete sentences, text completion and reading comprehension respectively. The paired and triple documents require the learner to connect specific details. [ETS TOEIC Listening & Reading](https://www.ets.org/toeic/about/listening-reading.html)
- The current TOEFL iBT Reading categories include Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life and Read an Academic Passage. The Atlas sets practise all three; they use a fixed question sequence. [ETS TOEFL iBT Reading](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/reading.html)
- The specialist-school collection samples selected advanced language-use and reading skills across possible school goals. Requirements depend on the school and examination year. The local mixed checkpoint samples skills rather than reproducing a school's paper. [VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted 2026–27 specifications](https://ptnk.edu.vn/cau-truc-de-thi-de-thi-minh-hoa-ky-thi-tuyen-sinh-vao-lop-10-truong-pho-thong-nang-khieu-nam-hoc-2026-2027/)

## Reviewing additions

Solve each item from the stimulus before comparing with the key. For a FALSE judgement identify a contradiction; for NOT GIVEN identify the missing evidence. Trace every completion to an exact source word. For paired documents distinguish a booking or cut-off time from the time of the event. For transformations reconstruct the sentence, check its meaning, word count and plausible alternatives. If several completions work, accept them or narrow the prompt. Check both explanations. Record independently solved answers in the release tests.

Published set IDs include an edition. Keep prior editions available for reviewing stored answers. Change the ID for a substantive scoring change. Practice events are scoped to the selected learner and enter existing export, restore and sync paths. They have no effect on CEFR placement or scheduled recall.
