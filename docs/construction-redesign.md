# Nim’s little harbor

The previous redesign still made answering activities the main action. This rebuild changes the core: choose an illustrated toy and tap a place to make it appear. Players can freely rearrange their harbor or act on a visitor’s short English request. Completion comes from what they build; there is no separate answer form, grid navigation or submit button.

We selected a construction toy over timed arcade play (speed would add pressure and motor demands) and branching narrative (more reading before meaningful agency). This is a design judgment, not evidence of superior learning outcomes. Luna’s limited primary-source review and format comparison are in `data/staging/workshop-briefs-review.md`.

Kids enters free construction. A grown-up can name the toys, follow the child’s interest and read optional requests. Balanced enters a single boat-on-water request, then progresses to two-object scenes and linked instructions. Both can freely build and retain creations in their journal. Serious enters direct A1–C2 study with its existing feedback, retrieval and review system. Modes share profiles and history; construction evidence remains distinct from assessed study evidence.

Nine original, individually reviewed requests cover estimated A1–B1 reading demands (3 A1, 4 A2, 2 B1). Nine inflated advanced proposals were rejected. This small toy cannot credibly assess advanced language; the unchanged 120 study activities across A1–C2 remain available through Study. Additional props, story contexts and richer language mechanics should be driven by observed play rather than inflating CEFR labels or request counts.

Every actual placement is an immutable saved event. Free play and each request use separate spaces. A matching snapshot earns one keepsake; moving or resetting it later preserves that accomplishment. Repeated saves/imports cannot multiply it. Hints, read-aloud and wrong target placements conservatively mark supported practice. Free decoration never creates independent reading evidence or proficiency awards. Shared-browser privacy, cloud ownership, rules and offline merge behavior remain unchanged.

The visual scene uses original SVG and native keyboard/touch buttons. No drag gesture, timer, required audio, new runtime service or paid dependency. English and Vietnamese guidance preserve the same in-hand selection and saved scene. Optional browser voices can be unavailable or require networking. Pre-readers need adult support for language learning; independent preschool suitability, enjoyment and efficacy have not been measured.

Verification covers actual UI placements, wrong-placement recovery, help, first and second requests, creative extras, separate saves, mode/language transitions, reload, duplicate backup imports and offline play. Screenshots at 1440/390/320 and axe checks supplement—not replace—human accessibility and enjoyment review. Production results are recorded in PROJECT_STATE after actual CI.
