## Ambiguity checks

Choice items are self-contained and use three or four distinct options; the explanations identify the decisive clue or form. Objective gaps constrain the requested word family, source form, or response length, with accepted answers listed in JSON. Ordering keys list token IDs in the required order.

For IELTS, the ten-day statement conflicts with the passage's three-day limit, whereas the funding claim is simply absent; paragraph B and C headings are supported by different central ideas, with two unused headings. All three completion answers are single words printed in the passage. The workplace notice explicitly states the move date, new room, contractor timing and adapter reply deadline. The Vietnamese word forms are fixed by bracketed bases; each transformation preserves its given keyword and exact word count. The listening prompts ask about details or inferences stated in the matching speaker script and have empty contexts as required.
